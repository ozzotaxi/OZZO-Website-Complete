How your Carmarthen taxi quote is estimated

The calculator uses the Google Maps driving distance and the selected date and time to apply the appropriate Carmarthenshire taxi fare tariff. Journey duration is shown for information only and is not charged as waiting time.

Tariff 16am–8pm

£4.50 first mile, then £0.25 for every additional 0.1 mile or part. Waiting is £0.30 per minute.

Tariff 28pm–midnight

£4.70 first mile, then £0.30 for every additional 0.1 mile or part. Waiting is £0.30 per minute. Also used on Sundays and public or bank holidays.

Tariff 3Midnight–6am

£5.15 first mile, then £0.35 for every additional 0.1 mile or part. Waiting is £0.40 per minute.

Christmas Day and New Year's Day use double Tariff 1. This gives a practical taxi quote for Carmarthen journeys while keeping the published charging rules clear.

Possible extras

These are not automatically added to your estimate because they depend on the individual booking:

  • Booking fee where the journey commences more than four miles from the operator's base: up to £5.00.
  • Vehicles carrying five to eight passengers: £1.00 for each passenger exceeding four.
  • Contamination or soiling charge: maximum £120. This is not included in a normal quote.
